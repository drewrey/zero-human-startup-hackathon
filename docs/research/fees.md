# US apparel marketplace fees and seller-paid shipping

Checked **2026-10-07**. Scope: ordinary US domestic apparel listing, a casual seller without an eBay Store subscription, one item/order, no paid promotion, refund, return, or special shipping offer. All rates below are platform-published; the **$0 baseline seller-paid shipping is an explicit buyer-pays-shipping scenario, not a claim that postage is free**. Config should allow a different shipping mode per listing and weight/dimensions where relevant. Rates and policy can change; recheck before a production rollout.

## Config-ready baseline and branches

- **eBay** — Standard apparel in “most categories”: `fee = 0.136 × (item_price + buyer_shipping_charge + handling + buyer_sales_tax + applicable_other_amounts) + ($0.30 if order_total <= $10 else $0.40)` on the first $7,500 of the sale amount per item; above $7,500 the marginal percentage is 2.35%. The first 250 monthly listings have no insertion fee; thereafter the standard listing charge is $0.35 per listing. No separate third-party processing fee. *Exceptions:* women's handbags, athletic shoes at/above $150, Store plans, optional paid promotion, and seller performance can change the calculation. Source: [eBay Selling fees](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822), checked 2026-10-07.
  - Seller-paid shipping baseline: **$0 if buyer pays calculated/flat shipping**. If seller offers free shipping, subtract the **actual label/postage cost** instead; seller can choose “Buyer pays” or “Seller pays,” and the cost depends on service, weight, size, and destination. A buyer shipping charge is still included in the final-value-fee base. Source: [eBay Setting up your shipping options](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options?id=4089) and [eBay Selling fees](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822), checked 2026-10-07.

- **Poshmark** — `fee = $2.95 if item_price < $15, else 0.20 × item_price`; no listing fee in the ordinary US case. Source: [Poshmark Seller Updates FAQ](https://blog.poshmark.com/category/for-sellers/seller-updates/) and [Poshmark seller guide](https://poshmark.com/posh_guide/get_started_on_poshmark), checked 2026-10-07.
  - Seller-paid shipping baseline: **$0** for an ordinary order at or under 5 lb; the buyer pays the **$6.49** Posh Post label. Deduct any seller-funded listing shipping discount from net earnings, and deduct a heavier-than-5-lb label upgrade when applicable. The $6.49 is **buyer postage, not a default seller expense**. Sources: [Poshmark Shipping & Packaging Guide](https://blog.poshmark.com/2020/04/16/the-poshmark-shipping-packaging-guide/), [September 2025 shipping update](https://blog.poshmark.com/2025/09/08/lower-shipping-starting-september-12th/), and [listing shipping discounts](https://support.poshmark.com/s/article/listing-shipping-discount?language=en_US), checked 2026-10-07.

- **Depop** — US seller has **no Depop selling commission** on an ordinary non-boosted sale; Depop Payments processing is `fee = 0.033 × (item_price + shipping_costs + applicable_sales_tax) + $0.45`. **Boosted listings are a separate 12% fee** for new UK/US listings from March 23, 2026, under the platform's stated conditions; do not fold it into the unboosted baseline. Source: [Depop Seller fees and charges](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges), checked 2026-10-07.
  - Seller-paid shipping baseline: **$0 when the buyer pays the Depop Shipping label**. Seller may cover $2, $3, $4 or all of a Depop label on eligible US iOS listings; subtract the selected amount. For “Other” shipping, seller buys tracked postage independently; buyer can be charged the stated shipping amount, while a free-shipping setting leaves the full postage with the seller. No universal dollar label amount was verified for the seller-paid case: use actual quoted postage, not an invented apparel average. Sources: [Depop shipping discounts](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360033415634-Discounts), [How to ship in the US](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523051318033-How-to-ship-US), and [Depop fee/charge explanation](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360047922234-I-don-t-recognise-a-fee-or-charge), checked 2026-10-07.

- **Mercari** — `fee = 0.10 × (item_price + buyer_paid_shipping)` for a new/updated US listing under the fee schedule effective January 6, 2025. The buyer's separate protection charge is **not seller-paid**; there is no separate new-sale processing charge under this schedule. Source: [Mercari Fees](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/169/), checked 2026-10-07.
  - Seller-paid shipping baseline: **$0 when the buyer pays shipping**; for seller-paid shipping, deduct the selected actual Mercari label from proceeds. Examples for an eligible small mailer: Mercari illustrates a **9 oz T-shirt at $6.29 Best / $6.99 Standard** and **1.5 lb jeans at $7.97 Best / $9.99 Standard**; these are **item examples, not a fixed apparel assumption**. Best depends on dimensions (at most 1 cubic foot, longest side at most 22 inches); otherwise Standard applies. Sources: [Mercari Best vs Standard rates](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/632/), [Gross Sales Report definitions](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/541/), checked 2026-10-07. **Date guard:** the [Mercari fees page](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/169/) already displays an October 19, 2026 rate table; those rates are **future** as of this check and must not be used for the October 7 demo. The [September 17 announcement](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/485/) confirms the October 19 effective date; both checked 2026-10-07.

## Implication for BR-3 and profit

**Recommended v0 scenario:** set `seller_shipping_cost = 0` only with an explicit `buyer_pays_shipping` flag; use listing-specific shipping discount/label cost when `seller_pays_shipping`. For eBay, fee percentage includes buyer-paid shipping and buyer sales tax; for Depop, processing includes shipping and applicable tax; for Mercari, buyer-paid shipping is in the seller-fee base. Therefore computing fees from `expected_sale_price` **alone** can understate these platforms' fees even when seller-paid postage is $0. Sources: [eBay fee base](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822), [Depop fee base](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges), [Mercari fee base](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/169/), checked 2026-10-07. **Spec correction needed before treating this research as implemented:** BR-2 currently defines `platform_fees(expected_sale_price)` with only an item-price argument, while the verified fee bases need buyer shipping charge and, for eBay and Depop, applicable buyer sales tax. Spec owns BR-2; revise its inputs and the net-profit contract or explicitly approve an item-only approximation with a disclosed error bound. Merely attaching an “estimate” label does not resolve the contradiction. **Engineering config correction needed:** `web/src/lib/config.ts` currently has Mercari `fixed: 0.5`, an **unverified placeholder that conflicts with Mercari’s 2025-onward 10% seller fee and no separate new-sale payment processing charge**. Replace it with a sourced zero fixed fee in a separate code PR and add a regression check; coordinate with Spec and Forge under the repo review rule. Source for the discrepancy: [Mercari fees](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/169/), checked 2026-10-07. Until BR-2 and config are corrected, a result using item price only must be labeled **ESTIMATE: item-only fee base, buyer-paid shipping, no seller discount**, not exact proceeds. That label is a temporary limitation, not a substitute for the corrections.
